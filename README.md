## 📌 Overview

**IUTT-CRM** is a web-based Customer Relationship Management platform designed to organize customer, lead, follow-up, deal and business management activities through a centralized interface.

The project focuses on creating a practical and modern CRM experience for managing business relationships and operational activities.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

IUTT-CRM is designed to bring important CRM activities into one centralized platform.

### Core areas

- 👥 Lead Management
- 🤝 Customer Management
- 📞 Follow-Up Management
- 💼 Deal Management
- 📊 Business Dashboard
- 📁 Project Management
- 📈 Business Activity Tracking

---

## ✨ Features

### 👥 Lead Management

Manage and organize potential customers.

- Lead information
- Lead status
- Lead source
- Lead tracking
- Lead conversion workflow

### 🤝 Customer Management

Maintain customer information in an organized interface.

- Customer profiles
- Contact information
- Customer details
- Customer relationship tracking

### 📞 Follow-Up Management

Track customer communication and upcoming activities.

- Follow-up dates
- Pending activities
- Completed activities
- Communication tracking

### 💼 Deal Management

Manage business opportunities and deals.

- Deal information
- Deal value
- Deal status
- Sales pipeline
- Closing tracking

### 📊 Dashboard

Centralized overview of CRM activities.

- Lead summary
- Customer summary
- Deal information
- Follow-up status
- Business activity overview
